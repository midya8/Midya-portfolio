# Midya Rahmani

Toronto-based technologist with 12+ years across technology, financial services, and telecommunications. Lately building with large language models, agentic systems, and workflow automation.

## What I build

- **LLM and agentic systems**: practical AI workflows, not demos. Things that do real work end to end.
- **Workflow automation**: taking manual, multi-step processes and making them reliable and repeatable.
- **Web platforms**: TypeScript, React, Next.js, and static-first architectures that stay fast and maintainable.

## How I build

The projects below share an approach, written up in [approach.md](approach.md): pick the simplest architecture that meets the requirement, make tools that work both in scripts and in the terminal, validate before executing, and treat reports and documents as outputs of the work rather than afterthoughts. Everything is built around real constraints, from right-to-left layouts to reduced-motion preferences.

## Projects
